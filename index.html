Who you're working with

A UF business student who got tired of watching good local businesses lose to worse ones that just showed up first on Google.

I'm Matthew Greco, a business student at the University of Florida born and raised in Jacksonville. I search Google constantly to find local restaurants and services, and the businesses that show up first aren't always the best ones, just the ones with their listing in order. That's true for every kind of business, not just restaurants.

I got into this while working for an AI consulting company during school, and after a few years managing local bars around Gainesville taught me how much a first impression matters. Local Boost FL is a one-person shop built on a simple idea: the best business in town shouldn't lose customers just because its Google listing is a mess. I handle every profile, every review reply, and every report myself, so nothing gets lost between you and the work.

What we actually do.

Most owners fill the profile out once and never touch it again. We treat it like a storefront window. Categories, services, service areas, hours, photos and posts, all set correctly and kept current.

  • Primary and secondary categories chosen from what actually ranks in your market
  • Complete services, service areas, attributes, and hours, holidays included
  • Fresh photos and weekly posts published for you
  • Questions and answers seeded with what customers really ask

Why it moves rank Category and completeness are two of the strongest local signals, and the two most often set wrong.

We write and publish a reply to every review you get, the good ones and especially the bad ones. Written to sound like you talking, not like a corporate script.

  • Replies to all new reviews within one business day
  • A tone guide built from how you actually talk to customers
  • Anything serious comes to you before it gets answered
  • Backfill of unanswered reviews from the last twelve months

Why it moves rank Reply text is indexed like any other content on your profile, and an answered profile reads as an active business.

Your business is listed in dozens of directories you have never logged into. An old suite number, a disconnected phone, a name from before the rebrand. We find every version and make them match.

  • Audit across 40 or more directories, data aggregators, and industry sites
  • Corrections submitted and tracked through to confirmation
  • Duplicate and unclaimed listings merged or removed
  • A quarterly re-check so it stays clean

Why it moves rank Conflicting information lowers confidence in your business data, and lower confidence means lower placement.

A simple system that asks happy customers at the right moment, after the job is finished rather than while you are still on site. No incentives and no gating, so nothing puts your profile at risk.

  • A branded review link plus printed QR cards for the counter or the truck
  • Text and email follow ups timed to your job cycle
  • A one page script so your crew knows when and how to ask
  • Monthly read on review volume, rating trend, and what customers keep mentioning

Why it moves rank Both count and recency feed local ranking. A profile earning reviews every week beats one that earned fifty in 2021.

One page, once a month. Where you rank for the searches that bring in paying work, how that changed, what we did, and what is next. No dashboard to log into and no jargon.

  • Grid ranking maps for your top search terms across the service area

We track the businesses outranking you across your service area and break down what they are doing differently. Categories, review pace, photos, posting habits, and where their listings are simply stronger than yours.

  • Up to five competitors tracked continuously
  • Side by side review count, rating, and growth rate

Why it moves rank Local ranking is relative. You do not need to be perfect, only better than the three businesses currently above you.
